## The game
You're Arthur Dent, and the game warns you straight away that it's going to be the worst day of your life. It follows the book's story loosely and is famous for being tricky and unfair in funny ways: it teases you, and the obvious thing often doesn't work. A perfect score is 400 points.

## Where you start
- In bed, in the dark: "You wake up. The room is spinning very gently round your head." You have a splitting headache and no tea.
- Bedroom: where you wake up, once you can see it. A mess: a washbasin, a chair with a tatty dressing gown, a window with the curtains drawn, and a phone near the exit to the south. A flathead screwdriver and a toothbrush are on the floor.

## Common questions
- Is that an error? Often it's a joke. Read what it says carefully: it usually tells you why something didn't work.
- HINT and HELP: the game only points to the printed hint booklet. No help there.
- DON'T PANIC.
