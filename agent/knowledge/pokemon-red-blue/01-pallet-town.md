# Pallet Town and the start of the game

## your house (upstairs)

Your bedroom, where the game starts. There's nothing you need here. The only
way out is the stairs in the top right corner (x 7, y 1): walk right along the
top of the room and step onto them to go downstairs. The PC on the left stores
items; you don't need it yet.

## your house (downstairs)

Mom is at the table: talk to her any time to heal your Pokemon for free. The
stairs up are in the top right. The way out is the door at the bottom of the
room (x 2 or 3, y 7): walk down onto the mat and keep pressing down to step
outside into Pallet Town.

## Pallet Town

A tiny town. Your house is the one on the left (door at x 5, y 5), your rival's
house is on the right, and Professor Oak's lab is the big building lower down
(door at x 12, y 11).

At the start, the lab is empty: Oak is out. To make the story move, walk north
to the top of town, where the path leads into the tall grass of Route 1. Oak
stops you before you reach the grass, then walks you to his lab himself. Just
press a through what he says (gb_read_on).

Later, the way north is Route 1, to Viridian City.

## Professor Oak's lab

Oak is at the top. On the table to the right of him are three Poke Balls, your
choice of first Pokemon: Charmander (left, x 6, y 3), Squirtle (middle, x 7)
and Bulbasaur (right, x 8). Stand below a ball, face up, press a, read what it
says and answer YES to take it. Your rival then picks the one that beats yours,
and battles you as you try to leave: pick FIGHT, then your first move, until
it's over. Win or lose, the story goes on.

Later Oak gives you the Pokedex here, after you bring him the parcel from the
shop in Viridian City. The way out is the door at the bottom (x 4 or 5, y 11).

## Route 1

The path north from Pallet Town to Viridian City. Tall grass has wild Pokemon:
fight them to make your team stronger, or walk around the grass. Keep going
north (up) to reach Viridian City.

## Viridian City

The first city. The Pokemon Center (red roof) heals your team for free: walk in
and talk to the nurse at the counter. The Poke Mart (blue roof) has a parcel
waiting for Oak: talk to the clerk, then take it back south to Oak's lab in
Pallet Town. After that, the way on is north to Route 2 and Viridian Forest, then
Pewter City and the first gym (Brock: Bulbasaur and Squirtle beat him easily,
Charmander needs a few more levels).
