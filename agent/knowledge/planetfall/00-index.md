## The game
You're an Ensign Seventh Class in the Stellar Patrol, the lowest rank there is, scrubbing the deck of the Stellar Patrol Ship Feinstein with your scrub brush while dreading Ensign First Class Blather. The title says where this is going: things go wrong and you end up on a planet. A perfect score is 80 points, and SCORE also tells you the day and the time.

## Where you start
- Deck Nine: a featureless corridor. It curves away to starboard, a gangway leads up, and to port is the entrance to one of the ship's primary escape pods (its bulkhead starts closed).
- You carry a Patrol uniform (worn) with an ID card in it, a chronometer (worn) and your scrub brush. EXAMINE them.

## Common questions
- Blather is shouting at me. He wants you at your post on Deck Nine, and he doesn't get less angry.
- What's the number on the status line? The time, not your moves: Galactic Standard Time from your chronometer.
- Am I all right? DIAGNOSE says how healthy, rested and fed you are.
