# Starcross: How to play
Audience: Luna, playing Starcross
Keywords: Starcross, commands, directions, fore, aft, port, starboard, parser, verbs, talking, computer, save, restore, wait, inventory, diagnose, complaints
Summary: How Starcross understands you, from its manual: plain English commands, ship directions, talking to characters with a comma, the one-word commands, and what each complaint from the game means and what to do about it.

## Moving
- On board ships: FORE (F), AFT, PORT (P) and STARBOARD (SB). NORTH, SOUTH, EAST and WEST (N, S, E, W) work too.
- UP (U), DOWN (D), IN and OUT work in some places.
- The manual says the alien ship has six directions plus IN and OUT, and that its geography is unusual. Note every room's exits.

## Sentences
- Type what you want to do, as if it started with "I want to": TAKE BOX, OPEN THE DOOR, PUSH THE RED BUTTON, LOOK UNDER THE TABLE, LOOK BEHIND THE PANEL, LOOK INSIDE THE CAGE, PUT IT ON.
- Several objects with AND or commas: TAKE THE AIR TANK AND PLIERS. ALL means everything visible that isn't inside something: TAKE ALL, DROP ALL EXCEPT THE PLAQUE.
- Several sentences on one line with THEN or a period, each still a turn. In your play turns, send one command at a time anyway.
- It reads only the first six letters of a word: SPACESUIT and SPACESHIP are the same word to it.
- Questions it understands: WHAT and WHERE only (WHERE IS THE ROBOT?).
- Saying something out loud: SAY "HELLO", ANSWER "A LASER".
- When it asks which one you mean ("the green panel or the blue panel?"), answer with just the missing word (GREEN) at the very next prompt.
- It guesses when only one thing fits, and tells you in brackets: TURN OFF (the force field).
- Words in a description that it doesn't recognise in your commands are only scenery.

## Talking to characters
Name, comma, then what you want them to do: COMPUTER, CONFIRM NEW COURSE. You can give one character several commands on one line. The ship's computer is the first one you'll talk to; it handles navigation and life support and has a surly streak.

## One-word commands
- LOOK (L): describe where you are in full.
- INVENTORY (I): what you're carrying.
- DIAGNOSE: a short medical report on yourself.
- WAIT (Z): let time pass without doing anything. Use it to see what something does, or where a moving thing goes.
- AGAIN (G): repeat the last command (not after talking to someone).
- SCORE: score, turns and your rank.
- VERBOSE, BRIEF, SUPERBRIEF: how much it describes rooms you've seen before. LOOK always gives the full description.
- SAVE, RESTORE, RESTART, QUIT, VERSION. Use your starcross_save and starcross_restore tools for saving rather than typing SAVE.

## When the game complains
- I DON'T KNOW THE WORD "...": not in its vocabulary. Try a synonym; if none works, the idea probably doesn't matter in the story.
- I CAN'T USE THE WORD "..." HERE: it knows the word as another part of speech. Rephrase.
- I CAN'T FIND A VERB IN THAT SENTENCE: every command needs a verb.
- I DON'T UNDERSTAND THAT SENTENCE: say it more simply.
- I THINK THAT SENTENCE WAS MISSING A NOUN: finish the sentence.
- I FOUND TOO MANY NOUNS IN THAT SENTENCE: split it into two commands.
- I CAN'T USE MULTIPLE OBJECTS WITH "...": one object at a time for that verb (TAKE, DROP and PUT take several).
- YOU CAN'T SEE ANY ... HERE: it isn't visible. It may be somewhere else, or inside something closed.
- YOU CAN'T GO THAT WAY: no exit that way.
- IT'S TOO DARK HERE TO SEE: you need light.
- I BEG YOUR PARDON?: you sent nothing.

## The manual's tips
- Draw a map: every room, its exits, and anything interesting there.
- Most things you can pick up matter, either for the goal or for a puzzle.
- Save often, and before anything dangerous or tricky.
- Read everything: room descriptions, objects, labels and signs hold clues. Even strange or dangerous actions can teach you something.
- There are many routes to the end. Some puzzles have more than one solution, and some don't need solving at all. Often one puzzle gives you what another needs.
- The memo in the box (03-the-story-so-far.md) asks you not to hurt any alien unless it's life or death.
