# Pokemon Red/Blue: how to play

Your guide to the start of the game. The sections below are named after the
places the game tells you you're in, and the right one comes to you each turn.

## Getting around

- Your look tells you where you are (x, y) and the ways out of the room, with
  the steps to each: "to your house (downstairs) at x 7, y 1 (6 right, 2 up)".
  Follow those steps. They're worked out from where you're standing right now.
- x grows to the right, y grows downwards. "up" makes y smaller.
- Stairs: walk onto them and you're taken to the other floor.
- Doors out of a building are at the bottom of the room: walk down onto the
  mat and keep pressing down until you're outside.
- Doors into a building: walk up into the door from the street.
- If a press didn't change your x or y, something is in the way (a wall, a
  table, a person). Go around it: try the other direction first, then come back.
- To talk to someone or read a sign: stand next to it, face it, press a.
