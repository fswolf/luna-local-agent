# Starcross: Notes index
Audience: Luna, playing Starcross
Keywords: Starcross, index, manual, star chart, mass detector, coordinates, Infocom, science fiction, help
Summary: Notes on Starcross, Infocom's 1982 science fiction adventure by Dave Lebling, written from the game's own manual and the papers that came in its box (starcross.pdf in this folder). No walkthrough and no puzzle answers: only what every player had in front of them.

## Files
- 01-how-to-play.md: How to talk to the game: directions on a ship, sentences it understands, talking to the computer, the one-word commands, and what its complaints mean.
- 02-star-chart.md: The mass detector printout from the box. It's how you set a course: the detector names a mass, the printout gives its coordinates, and you tell the ship's computer.
- 03-the-story-so-far.md: The world, your ship and your life before the game starts, from the captain's log and the Bureau of Extra-Solar Intelligence memo in the box.
- starcross.pdf: The scanned manual and box papers, for a person to read.

## The goal
Find the thing your mass detector picked up, get inside it, and learn to control it. The manual calls it a gargantuan alien spaceship. A perfect score is 400 points: points come from solving puzzles, getting treasures, doing certain things and reaching certain places, and you lose some for getting killed.

## Common questions
- Where do I start? Asleep in your bunk in the Living Quarters of your ship, the M.C.S. Starcross, with the mass detector's alarm ringing. Get up first (STAND), then the Bridge is starboard.
- How do I go anywhere in space? Read the mass detector on the Bridge, find that mass in 02-star-chart.md, and give the computer its coordinates. See 02-star-chart.md.
- Which way is which on the ship? FORE, AFT, PORT and STARBOARD (also N, S, E and W), plus UP, DOWN, IN and OUT. Room descriptions say which exits there are.
- Is there a map? Not in the box. The manual says to draw your own, because the alien ship's layout is unusual: write each room and its exits down with game_note as you go.
