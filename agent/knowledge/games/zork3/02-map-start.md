# Zork III: Map near the start
Audience: Luna, playing Zork III
Keywords: Zork III, map, Endless Stair, Junction, Creepy Crawl, Damp Passage, Barren Area, Foggy Room, Land of Shadow, Lake Shore, Cliff, Flathead Ocean, Royal Hall, Great Door
Summary: The rooms you can walk to from the start of Zork III without solving anything, with their exits and what the game says about the ways that don't work.

## Rooms
- Endless Stair: You are at the bottom of a seemingly endless stair, winding its way upward beyond your vision. Exits: south to Junction. Going north and up: "The stairs are endless."
- Junction: You are at the junction of a north-south passage and an east-west passage. Exits: north to Endless Stair, south to Creepy Crawl, east to Damp Passage, west to Barren Area.
- Creepy Crawl: You are in a dark and quite creepy crawlway with passages leaving to the north, east, south, and southwest. Exits: north to Junction, south to Foggy Room, east to Tight Squeeze, southwest to Land of Shadow.
- Damp Passage: This is a particularly damp spot even by dungeon standards. Exits: east to Dead End, west to Junction, northeast to Engravings Room. Going north: "The opening is blocked by tons of debris." Going south and up: "The channel is too steep and the moss too slippery."
- Barren Area: You are west of the junction, where the rock-bound passage widens out into a large, flat area. Exits: east to Junction, west to Cliff, northwest to Hairpin Loop, southwest to Land of Shadow. Going south and southeast: "A stone wall blocks your way."
- Foggy Room: You are in a dank passage filled with a wispy fog. Exits: north to Creepy Crawl, south to Lake Shore, west to Land of Shadow.
- Tight Squeeze: This is a very low and narrow passage leading east to west. Exits: east to Crystal Grotto, west to Creepy Crawl.
- Land of Shadow: You are at the eastern end of a dark, featureless landscape of shallow hills. Exits: east to Creepy Crawl, southeast to Foggy Room. Going north and northeast: "Towering walls of rock bar your way."
- Dead End: You have come to the end of two adjoining passages to the west and the northwest. Exits: west to Damp Passage, northwest to Engravings Room.
- Engravings Room: You are in a room with passages heading southwest and southeast. Exits: southeast to Dead End, southwest to Damp Passage.
- Cliff: This is a remarkable spot in the dungeon. Exits: east to Barren Area, southwest to Land of Shadow, down to Cliff Ledge. Going up: "There's no tree here suitable for climbing."
- Hairpin Loop: You are at a sharp turn on a narrow and steeply sloping path, strewn with boulders of various sizes. Exits: southeast to Barren Area, southwest to Cliff Base, up to Barren Area, down to Cliff Base.
- Lake Shore: You are in a wide cavern on the north shore of a small lake. Exits: north to Foggy Room, southeast to Aqueduct View, down to Aqueduct View. Going south: "If you really want to enter the lake, you should say so." Going southwest: "The sheer rock face prevents movement along the lake shore."
- Crystal Grotto: This is a chamber of breathtaking beauty. Exits: south to Royal Hall, west to Tight Squeeze.
- Cliff Ledge: This is a rock-strewn ledge near the base of a tall cliff. Exits: down to Cliff Base. Going up: "You attempt the climb but slide back down."
- Cliff Base: You are at the base of a steep cliff. Exits: south to Flathead Ocean, northeast to Hairpin Loop. Going up: "You can't get up the rock face."
- Aqueduct View: This is a small balcony carved into a near-vertical cliff. Exits: northwest to Lake Shore, up to Lake Shore. Going down: "The drop would be fatal."
- Royal Hall: This is the north end of a large hall with a vaulted ceiling. Exits: north to Crystal Grotto, south to Great Door.
- Flathead Ocean: You are at the shore of an amazing underground sea, the topic of many a legend among adventurers. Exits: north to Cliff Base, east to Land of Shadow, northeast to Land of Shadow, southeast to Land of Shadow. Going south: "The quicksand prevents movement here." Going west and northwest and southwest: "Between the rocks, wind, and waves, you wouldn't last a minute."
- Great Door: You are in the southern half of a monumental hall. Exits: north to Royal Hall. Going east: "The great iron door is rusted shut."

## How this map was made
By walking the game from the start with nothing but moves (after taking the lamp and turning it on), trying every direction in every room reached. Rooms behind a puzzle aren't here, and an exit a puzzle opens isn't either. The quotes are what the game said when that way didn't work. The game's own description wins if it disagrees.
