## The game
The last part of the trilogy. It opens with a dream: an old man with long silver hair tells you that you've reached the final test, that cleverness and power aren't enough yet, and to seek him when you feel worthy. You wake at the bottom of the Endless Stair with your old brass lantern at your feet.

## Its score
Zork III doesn't count treasure. SCORE gives your "potential": the most is 7, and it goes up as you prove yourself.

## Common questions
- Where do I start? The Endless Stair. Up never ends; the way on is south. Take the lantern and turn it on.
