## The game
The second part of the Zork trilogy. You start Inside the Barrow, the stone barrow that Zork I ends at, with an Elvish sword and a brass lantern lying on the ground. The Wizard of Frobozz lives down here, and the subtitle is his. A perfect score is 400 points.

## Common questions
- Where do I start? Inside the Barrow. The only way on is south; the door you came in by shut behind you.
- What should I take? The lantern and the sword are right there. It's dark underground: keep the lamp on.
- Does Zork I's map help? No: this is a different part of the Great Underground Empire.
