# Zork II: Map near the start
Audience: Luna, playing Zork II
Keywords: Zork II, map, Inside the Barrow, Narrow Tunnel, Foot Bridge, Great Cavern, Shallow Ford, Dark Tunnel, garden, Gazebo, Topiary, Carousel Room
Summary: The rooms you can walk to from the start of Zork II without solving anything, with their exits and what the game says about the ways that don't work.

## Rooms
- Inside the Barrow: You are inside an ancient barrow hidden deep within a dark forest. Exits: south to Narrow Tunnel. Going out: "You may not recall, but when you entered, the door of the barrow shut behind you."
- Narrow Tunnel: You are standing at the southern end of a narrow tunnel where it opens into a wide cavern. Exits: north to Inside the Barrow, south to Foot Bridge. Going down: "As you start down, you slip on the crumbling rocks and pull back, narrowly averting a fatal fall."
- Foot Bridge: You are standing on a crude but sturdy wooden foot bridge crossing a deep ravine. Exits: north to Narrow Tunnel, south to Great Cavern. Going down: "The drop would be fatal."
- Great Cavern: This is the center of the great cavern, carved out of the limestone. Exits: northeast to Foot Bridge, southwest to Shallow Ford.
- Shallow Ford: You are at the southern edge of a great cavern. Exits: north to Great Cavern, south to Dark Tunnel.
- Dark Tunnel: This is a dark tunnel with dim light to the northeast. Exits: northeast to Shallow Ford, southeast to North End of Garden, southwest to Path Near Stream.
- North End of Garden: This is the northern end of a formal garden. Exits: north to Dark Tunnel, south to Formal Garden, in to Gazebo.
- Path Near Stream: The path follows the south edge of a deep ravine and heads northeast. Exits: east to Formal Garden, northeast to Dark Tunnel, southwest to Carousel Room. Going down: "The ravine is extremely deep."
- Formal Garden: This is the middle part of a formal garden. Exits: north to North End of Garden, south to Topiary, west to Path Near Stream.
- Gazebo: This is a gazebo in the midst of a formal garden. Exits: out to North End of Garden.
- Carousel Room: You are in a large circular room whose high ceiling is lost in gloom. No plain way out. Going every direction: "You're not sure which direction is which." Going out: "Feeling dizzy, you pick a direction at random."
- Topiary: This is the southern end of a formal garden. Exits: north to Formal Garden, west to Carousel Room.

## How this map was made
By walking the game from the start with nothing but moves (after taking the lamp and turning it on), trying every direction in every room reached. Rooms behind a puzzle aren't here, and an exit a puzzle opens isn't either. The quotes are what the game said when that way didn't work. The game's own description wins if it disagrees.
