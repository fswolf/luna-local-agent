Connect Luna to a model

She thinks with a language model on a model server, and none is answering yet.

Looking for one now, every couple of seconds. She starts by herself as soon as one answers.

    On this computer: LM Studio

    The easiest way. Free, for Linux, Windows and Mac.

    1. Get it from lmstudio.ai and open it.
    2. Download a model: search for qwen3.5 9b (or 4b on a smaller graphics card) and load it.
    3. In the Developer tab, switch on Status: Running.

    That's all: Luna finds it on its own.

    On another computer

    A llama.cpp, LM Studio, Ollama or vLLM server on your network, like a home server.

    On this computer: llama.cpp

    Already have llama.cpp? Start llama-server on port 8080 with a model, and she finds it. On Linux, ./installer/install.sh --backend llama builds it for your graphics card.
